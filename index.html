HOW TO PLAY

🐘 Move Ganesha

Use Arrow Left / Right or touch ⬅️ / ➡️ to guide Lord Ganesha through sacred lands.

Press Space / Up Arrow or tap ⬆️ to leap across platforms and gather floating blessings.

🍬 Modaks & Flowers

Gather delicious modaks (+10) and fresh marigolds (+10) to honor the festival preparations.

⭐ Divine Energy

Collect 5 sacred glowing Divine Energy orbs in each realm to awaken ancient portals and shrines (+50).

🪔 Light Diyas

In the Festival City, touch the 5 sacred lamps to illuminate the path and dispel dark gloom (+20).

🧩 Sacred Puzzles

Solve path mazes, pattern sequences (🔔 🌺 🪔), and offering matching trials (+100).

✨ Wisdom Powers

Press E or tap ✨ to unleash Time Freeze, Obstacle Breaker, Double Score, Extra Life, or Divine Shield!
