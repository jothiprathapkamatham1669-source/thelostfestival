# 🐘 GANESHA – THE LOST FESTIVAL

A complete, polished, responsive 2D browser adventure and puzzle game celebrating **Ganesh Chaturthi**.

> **“Ganapati Bappa Morya! 🙏”**  
> *“Restore the festival. Solve the mysteries. Bring back the celebration!”*

---

## 🎮 Game Story & Objective

The festival of Ganesh Chaturthi is about to begin across the lands, but the **Divine Festival Energy** has mysteriously disappeared from the holy shrines and city streets!

Take control of **Lord Ganesha** (accompanied by his faithful mouse companion **Mooshika**) to explore four sacred realms, collect Divine Energy orbs, light holy diyas, unlock ancient sequence puzzles, and restore the central Maha Mandir altar to 100% celebration with grand Aarti and fireworks!

---

## 🌟 5 Playable Levels

1. **Level 1 — Sacred Forest**: Traverse ancient banyan tree platforms, collect 5 Divine Energy orbs, find hidden modaks and marigold flowers, avoid forest thorns, and solve the **Ancient Path Puzzle** to awaken the shrine portal.
2. **Level 2 — Festival City**: A vibrant Indian festival street with torans and rangolis. Light **5 unlit diyas**, gather offerings, dodge moving festival carts, and solve the **Festival Offering Matching Puzzle**.
3. **Level 3 — Ancient Temple**: Enter the sacred sanctum, leap to chime the temple bells, collect sacred symbols, and solve the missing symbol sequence puzzle (`🔔 → 🌺 → 🪔 → ?`) to slide open the giant temple gates.
4. **Level 4 — Divine Challenge**: A high-tempo obstacle course with moving chakras and fast-paced platforming. Unleash **Wisdom Powers** (Obstacle Breaker, Time Freeze, Double Score, Extra Life, and Divine Shield).
5. **Final Level — Restore the Festival**: The grand Maha Mandir Sanctum! Deposit the collected Divine Energy into the sacred Kalash altar. Watch the temple lights turn on, diyas ignite, flower petal showers pour down, and celebrate with grand fireworks and *"Ganapati Bappa Morya!"*.

## ⌨️ Controls

### Desktop Keyboard
- **Move Left / Right**: `Arrow Left` / `Arrow Right` or `A` / `D`
- **Jump**: `Space` or `Arrow Up` or `W`
- **Wisdom Power**: `E`
- **Pause & Back Menu**: `Escape` or `P` (or click `🏠 BACK` / `⏸️` in the HUD)

### Mobile & Tablet Touch
- Ergonomic on-screen virtual buttons:
  - ◀ **Left** and ▶ **Right**
  - ▲ **Jump**
  - ✨ **Wisdom Power**
  - **In-Game Back**: Tap the `🏠 BACK` button on the top HUD anytime to pause and return to the main menu.

## 🎵 Procedural Audio & Music Engine

Built with the **HTML5 Web Audio API** with zero external file dependencies:
- Melodic sitar and bansuri flute phrases tuned to festive Indian scales (Raag Bhupali).
- Resonant Tanpura drone and authentic Dholak / Tabla rhythms.
- Authentic sound effects: Temple bells (Ghanta), diya lighting chimes, conch blast (Shankha), fireworks, and modak collection.
